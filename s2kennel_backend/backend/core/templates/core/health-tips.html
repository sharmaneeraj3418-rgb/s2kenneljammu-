Dog & Pet Health Tips

Expert veterinarian guidelines and practical advice for keeping your puppy or kitten healthy, vaccinated, and happy.

Essential Puppy & Kitten Care Guide

Raising a healthy companion requires balanced nutrition, regular veterinary care, hygiene, and positive socialization. Follow these essential best practices recommended by S2 Kennel Jammu.

💉

Vaccination & Deworming Schedule

Early Weeks (6 – 8 Weeks)

Administer first puppy booster (DHPPi/L) and routine deworming drops under veterinary supervision.

Follow-up (10 – 14 Weeks)

Second vaccination booster, anti-rabies vaccine (at 3 months), and continuous monthly tick/flea prevention.

🥣

Nutrition & Meal Guidelines

High-Quality Protein

Feed age-appropriate starter food rich in DHA, calcium, and essential fatty acids for strong bone structure.

Clean Water 24/7

Always provide fresh, filtered drinking water. Avoid table scraps, chocolate, onions, and cooked bones.

🏃

Exercise & Mental Stimulation

Daily Play Sessions

20–30 minutes of moderate play prevents anxiety, destructive chewing, and obesity in growing puppies.

Positive Reinforcement

Use gentle reward-based training for potty habits, leash walking, and obedience commands.

🧼

Grooming & Coat Hygiene

Regular Brushing

Brush coat 2–3 times a week to remove loose fur and stimulate natural skin oils.

Ear & Dental Checkups

Clean ears with vet-approved wipes and maintain oral hygiene with safe chew toys.
