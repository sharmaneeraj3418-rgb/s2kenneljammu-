Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase a dog from S2 Kennel Jammu?

Contact us via WhatsApp or the enquiry form. We'll discuss your requirements, answer all questions, and arrange a visit to meet our dogs in person.

Do you provide health certifications?

Yes, all our dogs come with complete health certifications and records. We conduct thorough health checks before delivery.

What breeds do you currently have available?

We specialize in Labradors, Beagles, and Pomeranians. For current availability, please contact us directly or check our Instagram.

Do you provide after-sales support?

Yes, we provide lifetime support and guidance. You can reach out to us anytime for advice on training, health, or any concerns.

Can I visit the kennel?

Absolutely! We welcome visits during our working hours. Please call ahead to schedule an appointment.

What is your return policy?

We stand by our dogs completely. If for any reason you cannot keep your dog, we will take it back. We're committed to the wellbeing of all our dogs.
