About S2 Kennel Jammu

10+ Years of Dedication to Ethical Breeding, Certified Lineages, and Pure Pet Companionship in Vijaypur, Jammu & Kashmir.

Who We Are

Ethical Breeding & Health Standards

At S2 Kennel Jammu, our mission is to raise healthy, happy, purebred puppies and kittens with transparent lineage certificates, complete veterinarian health screening, and compassionate early socialization.

Located in peaceful Vijaypur along the Jammu highway, our breeding facility provides spacious suites, open outdoor play yards, and temperature-moderated living areas.

10+ Years of Dedication to Healthy & Ethical Pet Breeding

Harsh Sharma founded S2 Kennel Jammu with a vision to deliver genuine purebred companion dogs and cats with full health verification and lifetime breeder support. Under his guidance, hundreds of families across Jammu, Samba, Srinagar, and North India have welcomed lifelong furry family members.
